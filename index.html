About the Project

AMAI Green Atlas is a long-term biodiversity documentation initiative aimed at mapping trees, medicinal plants, rare species, and heritage trees throughout the Chengannur constituency. The project encourages participation from schools, eco-clubs, NSS units, local communities, and biodiversity enthusiasts.

🌳

Tree Mapping

Document and preserve important tree specimens throughout the constituency.

🌿

Medicinal Plants

Create a living digital catalogue of medicinal plants used in Ayurveda and traditional healthcare.

🏛️

Heritage Registry

Identify, protect and celebrate historically, culturally and ecologically significant trees.
